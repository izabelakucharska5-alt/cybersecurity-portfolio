## Overview

A cybersecurity exercise focused on documenting and analyzing a ransomware incident affecting a fictional healthcare organization.

The scenario explores how attackers gained initial access through a phishing attack, deployed ransomware and encrypted critical company files.

## Incident Analysis

The incident was documented using the **5 W's framework**:

- **Who:** An organized group of attackers.
- **What:** A ransomware attack.
- **Where:** A healthcare company.
- **When:** Tuesday, 9:00 AM.
- **Why:** Financial motivation, with attackers demanding payment in exchange for a decryption key.

## Key Discussion Points

- Identifying potential measures to prevent similar incidents.
- Understanding the impact of ransomware on healthcare organizations.
- Evaluating the risks and implications of paying a ransom.

## Skills Demonstrated

Incident documentation, ransomware analysis, security incident assessment and application of the 5 W's investigation framework
